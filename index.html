Lead store: the ₹199 consult and one test.

Why two

Bold claims stay off the main domain. A page with one button bounces less. It can test a higher price, and tracks separately.

What it costs

One customer becomes two records, so test-to-supplement can't be measured. Two pixels, split brand search, and 91% of referrals arrive on untagged bit.ly links.

The fix: one store, many doors

Every order on sova.health with one pixel. Funnel pages as templates (/score, /digestion, /consult) with no menu and one button. consultsova.com kept only as a page if it's a deliberate hedge. Tagged links, measured by entry page.

5

Why a test alone loses money, and what fixes it

One ₹3,999 order, line by line. Costs are my estimates until I see Sova's numbers; the shape is the point.

Assumes 18% GST and ₹1,500 to win a test buyer. Supplement line: 40% of buyers take a paid 3-month plan (₹699, then ₹899 twice), after ~₹315 product cost and ₹60 shipping a month. The first order earns ~₹1,100 before ads and loses money after them; the supplement plan is what makes a buyer profitable.

What one kit costs to move

Box, stabiliser tube, scoop, gloves, return pouch₹200 to ₹350
Forward courier, 0.5 kg₹50 to ₹90
Scheduled reverse pickup, biological sample₹120 to ₹250
Paid before the lab even starts₹370 to ₹690

Where it leaks, and the fix

Kits never sent backBook the pickup slot at checkout; WhatsApp the 2-day window with a 60-second video
Failed pickupsPrice a lab partner's home-collection fleet against couriers in the top three metros
COD refusalsTests prepaid only, or a ₹199 advance
